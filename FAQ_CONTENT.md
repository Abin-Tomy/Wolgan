## Chemical Supplies

### Q1 — What water treatment chemicals does Wolgan supply?

Wolgan supplies a comprehensive range of water treatment chemicals including corrosion inhibitors, scale control agents, microbiological growth control products, flocculants, coagulants, and pH adjusters. Commodity chemicals supplied include caustic soda solution, sodium hypochlorite, calcium hypochlorite, sulphuric acid, sodium meta bi-sulphate, and soda ash light.

---

### Q2 — What is antiscalant used for in water treatment?

Antiscalant is a chemical added to water systems to prevent the formation of mineral scale on membranes, heat exchangers, and pipework. It works by sequestering calcium, magnesium, and other scale-forming ions. Wolgan supplies and doses antiscalant as part of water treatment chemical programmes.

---

### Q3 — What causes legionella in water systems and how is it controlled?

Legionella bacteria thrive in warm stagnant water in cooling towers, hot water systems, and spa pools. Control methods include maintaining water temperatures outside the growth range, regular disinfection using biocides, and periodic hyperchlorination. Wolgan supplies legionella control chemicals and provides water treatment programmes compliant with regional health guidelines.

---

### Q4 — What is the difference between a corrosion inhibitor and a biocide?

A corrosion inhibitor is a chemical that forms a protective layer on metal surfaces to prevent corrosion in water systems. A biocide is a chemical that kills or controls microorganisms such as bacteria, algae, and fungi. In cooling water and chilled water treatment programmes, both are typically used together to control scaling, corrosion, and fouling.

---

### Q5 — What is odour control in water and wastewater treatment?

Odour in water and wastewater systems is typically caused by gases produced by bacteria in low-oxygen conditions. Odour control chemicals are dosed to neutralise these compounds. Wolgan supplies odour control chemicals and provides ongoing dosing programmes for sewage treatment plants, pump stations, and drainage networks.

## MEP Installations

### Q1 — What does MEP installation include?

MEP installation covers mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems for buildings and infrastructure. Wolgan's scope includes chilled water system installation, HVAC systems, plumbing (hot and cold water, drainage, sanitary), energy transfer stations, district cooling connections, firefighting systems, and process piping. We serve commercial, hospitality, healthcare, and industrial projects across Qatar and UAE.

---

### Q2 — What is chilled water flushing and why is it necessary?

Chilled water flushing is the process of circulating clean water through a new or refurbished pipe system to remove debris and residue before the system goes live. It prevents blockages, valve damage, and corrosion. Wolgan performs chilled water flushing and chemical cleaning as part of MEP commissioning services.

---

### Q3 — What is passivation in piping systems?

Passivation is a chemical treatment applied to metal pipework after cleaning to form a protective layer on the internal surface. This layer prevents corrosion and extends the life of chilled water and heating systems. Wolgan carries out passivation as a standard step in MEP commissioning and new system handover.

---

### Q4 — What is a district cooling plant and how is it installed?

A district cooling plant produces and distributes chilled water from a central facility to multiple buildings through insulated underground pipes. Installation involves the distribution network, pumping stations, energy transfer stations, and mechanical plant room equipment. Wolgan has extensive experience in district cooling infrastructure installation across Qatar.

## Water Treatment

### Q1 — What is reverse osmosis and how does it work?

Reverse osmosis is a water purification process that uses a membrane to remove dissolved salts, bacteria, and other contaminants. Water is forced under pressure through the membrane, leaving impurities behind. Wolgan designs and installs reverse osmosis systems for industrial and commercial applications across Qatar, UAE, and India.

---

### Q2 — How much does a water treatment plant cost?

The cost of a water treatment plant depends on capacity, treatment type, and site conditions. A basic system may start from AED 50,000, while full sewage or effluent treatment plants for large facilities can range significantly higher. Contact Wolgan for a customised quotation.

---

### Q3 — What is included in a water treatment Annual Maintenance Contract?

A water treatment Annual Maintenance Contract from Wolgan includes scheduled inspections, chemical dosing adjustments, filter media replacement, equipment servicing, water quality testing, and emergency callout support. Contracts are tailored for cooling towers, reverse osmosis plants, sewage treatment plants, chilled water systems, and boiler systems.

---

### Q4 — What is a sewage treatment plant and how does it work?

A sewage treatment plant processes wastewater from buildings to remove solids, organic matter, and harmful organisms before discharge or reuse. The treatment process involves screening, biological treatment, clarification, and disinfection. Wolgan designs, installs, and operates sewage treatment plants for hotels, hospitals, and industrial sites.

---

### Q5 — What is grey water treatment and can grey water be reused?

Grey water is wastewater from sinks, showers, and laundry, excluding toilet waste. After treatment through filtration and disinfection, grey water can be safely reused for irrigation, toilet flushing, and cooling tower top-up. Wolgan installs grey water recycling systems to reduce freshwater consumption and support sustainability targets.
